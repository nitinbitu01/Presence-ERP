# Security Policy & Responsible Disclosure

## Reporting Security Vulnerabilities

Rashtriya Raksha University (RRU) and the Presence ERP Engineering Team take security seriously. If you discover a vulnerability in Presence ERP, please report it responsibly.

### Contact
- **Email**: `security@rru.ac.in`
- **Response Commitment**: Initial acknowledgment within 24 hours; status updates every 72 hours.

---

## Scope & Guidelines
- Do not access or modify data belonging to other accounts.
- Do not perform Denial of Service (DoS) testing against production infrastructure.
- Execute security research strictly against local development environments or designated staging sandboxes.
